WHO WE ARE

Welcome to RELIVE

RELIVE – Give Fashion Another Chance is a sustainability-focused student project designed to encourage responsible clothing habits. Our platform promotes reusing, repairing, recycling and donating clothes instead of throwing them away.

🌱

Our Mission

To reduce textile waste by encouraging people to extend the life of their clothing through reuse, repair, recycling and donation.

🌍

Our Vision

To create a community where sustainable fashion becomes an everyday habit and every garment gets the opportunity for a second life.
